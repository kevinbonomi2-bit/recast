| Library | Used for | License |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Inspiration

Recast's drag, tap and drop workflow is inspired by [Tangerine](https://tangerineformac.com/) for Mac. Recast is an independent project written from scratch and contains no code, artwork or other assets from Tangerine.
